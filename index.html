About me

I am a mechanical engineering student at Chennai Institute of Technology who works at the intersection of aerodynamics, design and manufacturing. I build my own aircraft in SolidWorks, test them virtually in ANSYS Fluent, and then take them from the screen to the field.

My focus is CFD: airfoils, wing and fuselage configurations, propeller thrust, noise reduction and indoor airflow. I also bring shop-floor exposure from engine assembly, injection moulding and engine test-rig vibration analysis.
